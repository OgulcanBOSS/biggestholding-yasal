Privacy Policy — AuraBOSS

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how AuraBOSS ("the App," "we," "us," or "our")
handles information when you use our mobile application. AuraBOSS is developed
and operated by [COMPANY NAME] ("the AuraBOSS team").

By using AuraBOSS, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do
not agree, please do not use the App.

1. What Information We Collect

1.1 Photos (temporary, not stored)

When you take or upload a selfie for face analysis, the photo is sent to a
third-party AI provider (see Section 2) for processing. The photo is used only to
generate your analysis results. We do not store your photos. Once the
analysis is complete and the results are returned to your device, the photo is
not retained on our servers.

1.2 Selections you make (temporary)

Information you provide during setup, such as gender selection or your
self-improvement goals, is used to personalize your results and is processed
in the same temporary manner as your photo. It is not retained beyond what is
needed to generate and display your results.

1.3 Purchase / subscription status

We (and/or the App Store / Google Play, and our subscription/payment processing
provider) keep a record of your subscription status — for example, whether you
have an active subscription — so the App can unlock the correct features. We do
not receive or store your full payment card details; these are handled directly
by Apple or Google.

1.4 Analytics

As of this version of the App, AuraBOSS does not use any analytics or
tracking service. If we add analytics or crash-reporting tools in the future,
this Privacy Policy will be updated in advance to describe exactly what is
collected and why, and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

2. Third-Party AI Processing

To generate your face analysis and glow-up recommendations, your photo is
transmitted to a third-party AI provider — currently Anthropic — for
processing. The provider analyzes the image and returns the results to the App.
We do not control how long the third-party provider's infrastructure may
transiently hold data during processing, but AuraBOSS itself does not store or
archive your photo before, during, or after this exchange.

3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above only to:

  • Generate your AI face rating and glow-up plan.
  • Personalize the guidance shown to you in the App.
  • Manage your subscription and unlock paid features.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention

  • Photos: not stored. Used only for the duration of the analysis request.
  • Selections/preferences: not retained beyond what is needed to generate and

display your results, unless you have chosen to save preferences locally on
your own device.

  • Subscription status: retained for as long as needed to manage your

subscription and comply with legal/accounting obligations.
